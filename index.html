FAQ

How do I restore my Pro purchase?
Open the app → Settings (gear icon) → Restore purchase. Use the same Apple ID / Google account you bought with.

Is this gambling?
No. Ace of Hearts is a training tool. There is no real money, no chips and no prizes.

Is my data private?
Your progress stays on your device. See the privacy policy.
