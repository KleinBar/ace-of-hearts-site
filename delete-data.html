Delete your data

Ace of Hearts: Poker Trainer – published by Hula Play (Google Play) and Aviad Klein (App Store).

The app has no accounts. Most of your data never leaves your phone, and the rest is anonymous and deleted automatically.

1. Data on your device

Scores, streaks, skill ratings, the review list and settings are stored only on your device.

  • To delete it: open the app → Settings (gear icon) → Reset all progress, or simply uninstall the app.
  • It is deleted immediately and cannot be recovered.

2. Anonymous usage data and crash reports

If usage data is on, the app sends anonymous usage events (Firebase Analytics) and crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics). They are tied to a random app-instance ID, not to your name, email or advertising ID.

  • To stop it: Settings → turn off Share anonymous usage data.
  • To request deletion of data already sent: email pokerninjateam@gmail.com with the subject “Ace of Hearts data deletion”. We will delete what we can match and confirm within 30 days.
  • Even without a request, analytics data is deleted automatically after 2 months and crash reports after about 90 days.

3. Purchases

Pro purchases are handled by Apple / Google and verified through RevenueCat using an anonymous ID. Purchase records are kept as long as Apple, Google and tax law require, so that you can restore your purchase. Your payment details are never shared with us.
